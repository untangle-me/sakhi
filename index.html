Our Story

The Beginning

Why We Started
Sakhi Collective

It started with a simple frustration: finding the perfect accessories to complete an outfit was always a struggle. Hours spent in crowded markets, mismatched pieces arriving at the doorstep — there had to be a better way.

Sakhi Collective was founded with one clear mission — to make Indian women feel effortlessly put-together. Every woman deserves to shine at her sister's wedding, her cousin's festival celebration, and every moment in between.

We partner with talented local artisans and vendors from across India — women entrepreneurs, home-based crafters, and small boutique owners — to bring you pieces that carry both quality and soul.
